The Sentry Man Appears

Your camera lens is severely cracked and your vision blurs with heavy static. Right in front of you, an Astro Toilet dives down at blinding speed with its laser blade unsheathed, ready to smash your camera to pieces. You can only lie there, completely helpless.

"CRASH!"

Suddenly, a gleaming steel sword flashes through the air, slicing the Astro Toilet clean in half before it erupts into a violent explosion!

Through the smoke, a heavily armored soldier with a distinct, blocky mechanical head steps forward, shielding you. Engraved on his chest plate is the emblem of a brand-new faction: Sentry Man. He smoothly spins his sword, turns around, and extends a metallic hand down toward your camera.

Your lens captures his powerful first appearance, and the screen slowly cuts to black.
